Behaviour
Select an aircraft by clicking its marker on the map or its name in the timeline. If its lane has data at the cursor, the cursor stays where it is. If not, the cursor jumps to its first frame (cursor before the lane) or last frame (cursor after it). Follow keeps the map centred on the selected aircraft; dragging the map turns Follow off. Click the timeline to move the cursor.
